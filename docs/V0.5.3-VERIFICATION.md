# Hearthfield v0.5.3: self-care and resource traversal

## Root causes demonstrated before implementation

Three new tests failed on deployed v0.5.2 code: a pawn surrounded by four resource stacks had no exit path; a hungry pawn with a meal chose `separate`; successful personal cooking left `pawn.job` null instead of eating. These same tests now pass.

The scheduler sorted ascending: lower numbers won. Need candidates bypassed work priorities and used their score directly. Ordinary work used `priority * 100 + distance + score - skill * 2` (priority zero disabled it). Eating began below hunger 38, personal cooking below 35; ordinary interruption began below 20.

The former food ordering was generic separation (-1200), personal cooking (-1100), then eating (-1000 + distance, with meal -40, berries -10, other emergency raw +20). The fixed constants were an attempted urgency ordering, not an inverted comparator. They explicitly put waste handling and new recipes ahead of prepared meals. Only two candidate path attempts could then exhaust the scan before a meal was tried. Cooking was exempt from hunger interruption, and cooking completion released all locks and returned to unrestricted selection. Together these explain both ignoring an existing meal and finishing a meal without eating it.

## Corrected behavior

Assignment first orders semantic tiers: prepared meal, eligible directly edible emergency food, personal food preparation, rest, ordinary work. Existing score formulas only break ties inside tiers; there is no global score inversion. Food candidates have reservation/path checks and are not cut off by the two-attempt work budget. Duplicate candidate keys are attempted once per scan. The existing ten-second retry cooldown remains for genuinely unreachable targets.

Personal cooking counts reachable, unreserved fresh ingredients before admission. Mixed ingredients may be selected: their existing three-second separation action runs at the source while the cooking job keeps its station/source ownership. Waste stays physical and subsequent Haul work takes it to a Dump. Generic separation is ordinary Haul work with score 50, never an independent hunger override.

Assigned personal cooking carries `personalFoodPlan`. On success it claims the exact newly created meal and replaces the cook job with an eat job synchronously, retaining the transaction ID. There is no scheduling gap, storage trip, or output theft. Preparation and eating still take their normal time.

Every needs update, a pawn below hunger 20 can preempt any non-eat job for a reachable, unreserved prepared meal. `interruptJob` refunds `ingredientFresh` as physical raw food at the station, resets its buffer/progress/owner, drops carried cargo, and releases the old locks; then the pawn claims the meal. The regression conserves 82 buffered + 7.47 carried + 50 source = 139.47 raw points. Ingredients are recovered physically rather than parked in a suspended job. A completed or nearly completed recipe may also be refunded if a ready meal is available at this critical check; this favors the ready meal and does not duplicate either recipe.

Navigation no longer marks resource entities blocked, `walkable()` no longer rejects items, and pickup/harvest/deposit no longer mutate item collision cells. Adjacent item interaction is retained. Deep water, completed walls and solid natural nodes (trees/outcrops) remain blocking. Doors, beds, crops, fields and blueprints retain their intended traversal. Construction placement still rejects occupied item tiles. Storage/stack merging, item IDs, reservations, hauling, spoilage, expiry and quantities remain physical.

An additional stale branch used four raw points when hunger exceeded 35. It now consistently uses the existing `COOKING_INPUT = 100` constant. The corresponding old four-point recipe test was corrected. Crop yield/growth, hunger/rest drains, starvation damage, food/waste caps, carrying capacities, spoilage/weather/shelter modifiers, waste expiry and mood effects were not changed.

## Diagnostics and saves

Added `FOOD_SELFCARE_EVALUATED`, `FOOD_PLAN_MEAL_SELECTED`, `COOK_TO_EAT_TRANSITION`, and `SELFCARE_PREEMPTED_JOB`. Existing events remain; consumption includes food type. Unneeded fallback checks are reported as null (not evaluated), rather than false. Debug reports include each meal's edible/reachable/available state, reservation owner, path cost per pawn, and expiry timestamp. No per-movement logging was added.

Save schema remains **5**. No resources are moved or deleted on load. Existing dense storage becomes traversable through the rebuilt runtime grid. Jobs remain ephemeral under existing load/refund rules; the optional personal-plan/substep fields need no migration.

## Deterministic integration evidence

`tests/sim/selfcare-long-run.test.ts` runs 18,000 ticks (three days), three colonists, a 3×3 renewable field, cooking station, three beds, construction, Dump, spoilage/weather and a three-tile-wide resource band spanning the entire 20×20 map. Counters observe the complete event stream, not only the 5,000-event diagnostic ring or 40-entry journal.

Seven meal entities remain at the end (3 starting + 10 produced - 6 consumed); early stored meals have expired under the unchanged meal lifetime. No pawn reached zero hunger while an accessible edible meal was available. The scenario does not claim that all stored meals will be consumed before expiry or that farming is balanced.

Food accounting includes raw fresh+spoiled points, waste, carried cargo, station buffers and meals valued at 80. The final physical total equals starting 465 + harvests 3150 - cooking conversion loss 200 - consumption 480 - expired waste 454.00068149190423, within 1e-6. Each consumed meal ID is unique. This detects duplication and unexplained loss separately from intended consumption/conversion/expiry.

The observed instrumented scenario took approximately 2.4 seconds locally (~0.13 ms per tick including observers). This is a local test measurement, not a mobile-device benchmark. A separate enclosed-meal regression produces exactly one real path failure during the first 99 ticks, respects cooldown, and selects another accessible meal immediately when it appears. No percentage reduction against the unavailable full live trace is claimed; the supplied failure patterns and boxed-pawn reproduction are eliminated in the tested layouts.

## Regression and release checks

New coverage: meal precedence, uninterrupted cook/eat ownership, critical ordinary-work and ingredient-gathering preemption, exact refunds, required-vs-generic separation, reserved/unreachable meal fallback, cardinal-box escape, full-stockpile/farm crossing, retained wall/water/node collision, physical storage/spoilage/expiry, genuine topology cooldown, construction material reachability, and schema-5 dense-layout loading. Existing v0.5.1 multi-source/reservation/waste tests and v0.5.2 logistics tests remain in the suite. Superseded solid-item and four-point-recipe expectations were updated deliberately. The v0.5.2 integration test now records cooking throughout its run so journal eviction cannot erase the evidence.

Release gates: full simulation suite (74 tests), TypeScript, formatting, production build, PWA verifier, complete browser suite (12 tests), and live Chromium Pages smoke. Exact deployment commit/build is reported in the release response. The reusable live check is `node scripts/smoke-live.mjs URL VERSION BUILD`.

## Implementation map

- `src/sim/job-assignment.ts`: `assignJob` semantic precedence, candidate deduplication, reachable ingredient feasibility, station claim, personal-plan marker and selection tracing.
- `src/sim/job-board.ts`: `needCandidates` offers mixed personal ingredients and alternate sources; `workCandidates` owns generic separation.
- `src/sim/jobs.ts`: `advanceJob` required separation, exact 100-point recipe, reachable source rotation, synchronous output claim/eating, and removal of resource-grid writes. Existing `interruptJob` performs refund recovery.
- `src/sim/selfcare.ts`: shared `preparedMealOptions`/`reachableMeal` for preemption and diagnostic exports.
- `src/sim/simulation.ts`: `Simulation.step` critical meal preemption.
- `src/sim/pathfinding.ts` and `src/sim/world.ts`: resource-independent `navigationGrid`/`walkable`.
- `src/sim/types.ts`: optional runtime personal-plan and separation-progress fields.
- `src/sim/diagnostics.ts`: `buildDebugReport` meal accessibility and `diagnosticItem` expiry.
- New `selfcare-pathing` and `selfcare-long-run` simulation tests; adjusted `friction`, `survival`, `storage-throughput` and browser version expectations.
- `scripts/smoke-live.mjs`, version metadata (`src/build.ts`, package manifests), README, architecture and implementation-status documentation.
